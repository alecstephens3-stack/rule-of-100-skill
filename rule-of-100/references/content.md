# Free content: Hormozi's method

A summary of the free content chapter of *$100M Leads*, from the transcript linked in `the-rule.md`. The daily 100 here is 100 minutes spent making content, with at least one piece published that day.

## The content unit: hook, retain, reward

- **Hook.** Give them a reason to stop. He builds hooks from a topic (something from your far past, your recent past, the present, a trend, or something you set up on purpose), a headline, and a format that matches what already does well on the platform.
- **Retain.** Keep them with lists, steps or stories.
- **Reward.** Deliver what the hook promised. He measures content by how much value it gives per second.

Long content is several of these units linked together. He says to start short.

## Giving and asking

- His rule is to give until they ask, and to give in public and ask in private.
- When you do ask, ask for one thing at a time. If you're not sure whether to promote the main offer or a free lead magnet, he says to use the lead magnet.
- An ask can sit inside a piece, after a valuable moment or at the end, or come as its own post after a run of giving posts.

## What to do first

- Post the lessons and the results from your first customers.
- Keep doing warm reach-outs alongside. Content is added, not swapped in.
- Say "how I" did something, which you can prove, instead of "how to".
- Make every piece for strangers, so someone who has never heard of you can follow it.
- Stay narrow before going broad.
- Repeat yourself. Most people never saw it the first time.

## Mistakes he names

- Making content only insiders understand.
- Asking too often.
- Going broad too early.
- Scheduling posts ahead and walking away from them.

## His benchmarks

He gives no reply-rate benchmark for content the way he does for reach-outs. For the weekly review, track minutes done, pieces published, and the lead measure the user chose (inbound messages, lead magnet requests, calls booked), and use the first two weeks as the baseline.

## What this means for the skill

- The 100 minutes are the person's. Only time spent making counts: writing, recording, editing. Scrolling for ideas, answering comments and checking stats don't count.
- The skill's job is to make sure none of the 100 minutes go to deciding what to make. Each day it has ready a short queue of topics drawn from the user's own work and results, each with a hook, the list, steps or story that carries it, and the reward.
- A day counts only if the minutes were done and at least one piece went out. Log the minutes as the count and the link to the published piece as the proof.
- Where the session has the tools and the go-ahead, the skill can cut, caption and post what the person made. It does not invent results or stories for them.
