# Cold reach-outs: Hormozi's method

A summary of the cold outreach chapter of *$100M Leads*, from the transcript linked in `the-rule.md`. Cold means strangers. He suggests doing warm reach-outs and content first, for the practice.

## The three problems and his fix for each

1. **You have no way to contact them.** Build a list. Try software that gathers contacts first, then list brokers, then finding people by hand in groups and communities. With more time than money, start by hand. Put the first 1,000 names together before anything else.
2. **They ignore you and don't trust you.** Personalize, then give big fast value. Personalizing means one to three things about the person that a friend might know. Big fast value means giving away something people normally pay for.
3. **Few respond.** Volume: automate what you can, and follow up more times and in more ways.

## His numbers

- Don't set a daily goal below 100, and don't stop for 100 days minimum.
- Follow-up: twice on day one, twice on day two, once on day seven.
- Work the list again after three to six months.
- Get the first 100 conversations, or the first 10,000 emails, done before changing the script.
- He says you are doing well when a customer's lifetime profit is at least three times what it cost to get them.

## His benchmarks

- **Calls:** 100 calls, about a 20% pick-up rate, and about 25% of those want the lead magnet. He gives the result as four engaged leads. (The two percentages multiply to five. Four is the figure in the reading, so treat it as roughly four to five.)
- **Email:** 100 personalized emails, about 30% opened, about 10% of those reply with interest. That is about 3 per 100.

Use these in the weekly review to find the weak step. A low open or pick-up rate points at the list, the subject line or the time of day. Opens without replies point at the message or the offer.

## Mistakes he names

- Underestimating how much volume and time it takes. It took him close to a year to make it work.
- Not tracking the numbers.
- Contacting people again after they said no.

He notes that he left the word-for-word scripts out of the book, so there is no Hormozi script to copy. Write the user's own.

## What this means for the skill

- Cold is where the skill can do the most itself, and where the most can go wrong. Before the first send, look up the sending limits and automation rules of each platform, and the law on unsolicited messages for the user's country and the recipients' (for example CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR and ePrivacy rules in Europe, CASL in Canada, and telephone rules for calls and texts). Put what you found, with the date and the source, in `run.md`.
- New sending addresses and accounts can't send 100 on day one without landing in spam. If the user is starting from nothing, say what the real number is this week and when it reaches 100.
- Anyone who says no, unsubscribes or bounces goes on a do-not-contact list that every later day checks.
- Keep his follow-up cadence in a queue, and decide at setup whether follow-ups count toward the 100.
