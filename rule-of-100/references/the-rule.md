# The Rule of 100: what Hormozi says, and how sure we are

Read this before explaining the rule to anyone or citing it.

## Where this comes from

## What he says

- The rule: advertise by doing 100 primary actions every day for 100 days in a row. Do that, he says, and you will get more engaged leads.
- Per channel:
  - Warm reach-outs: 100 a day. Email, text, direct message, calls.
  - Cold reach-outs: 100 a day. Email, text, direct message, cold calls, flyers. He adds to use automation, because response rates are lower. In the cold chapter he also says not to set a daily goal below 100 and not to stop before 100 days.
  - Free content: 100 minutes a day making it, and at least one piece released a day on a platform. As you get better, post more.
  - Paid ads: 100 minutes a day making ads, run on the daily budget worked out in his paid ads chapter.
- One, not all: you only need one of the four to get engaged leads, so pick one and max it out. Choose by what you have. More time than money points to content. More money than time points to cold reach-outs or paid ads.
- Why: the simplest way to succeed in marketing is to take a lot of actions. He asks what is stopping someone from doing ten times what they do now.
- What comes after: more, better, new.
  - More is volume, up to full capacity.
  - Better is testing one thing per week per platform.
  - New is new placements, then new platforms, then another of the four channels.
  - Under $1M a year in profit, more comes first. Above it, better comes first.
- His action step is to get it going, measure how you do, and scale up from there.

## Other people's versions (not in the book as read)

- "$100 a day on ads" is how summaries often state the ads version. His unit is minutes spent making ads.
- "At least 90 days" appears on fan sites. He says 100 days in a row.

## What he never addresses

- Days off, missed days, restarting, or what counts as failing the rule. The rules this skill uses for those are its own.
- A promise about when results show up.
- Using the rule for anything other than advertising. This skill will run it on any repeatable action, and says so when it does.
